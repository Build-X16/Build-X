Returns

Unused parts in their original packaging can be returned within 14 days of delivery. Email us your order details and we reply with a return address. Refunds go back to your original payment method within 7 days of receiving the parcel.

Damaged or wrong item? Send a photo within 7 days and we send a replacement.

Questions

Will I pay customs or import tax?

Possibly. Duties depend on your country and are paid by the buyer on delivery.

Can I change my address after ordering?

Yes, until the parcel ships. Email us as soon as you can.

Do you ship batteries?

Only battery holders. Buy AA cells locally, since lithium cells need special shipping.
